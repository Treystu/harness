### What the cure actually was, and one trap worth recording

Most sites were **test doubles and deliberately wrong values** - `BrokenServer()` and `SimpleNamespace` assigned to `_ValidatedEgressProxy._server`/`_thread`, `finish_payload`, `recover_interrupted=cast(bool, "yes")`, the finished-state dict a resumed run replays. Those got `cast(...)`, which states the intent (this is a fake / this value is wrong on purpose) instead of suppressing the check. Two were real contract corrections: `Route(...)` takes its `offer` as the second optional (the mocked path reads only `.provider`), and `is_read_only_audit_request` already handles `None` internally via `prompt or ""` - so the annotation, not the test, was wrong.

**The trap:** the obvious place to fix the two `lifecycle` errors was `harness/task_lifecycle.py`, declaring `lifecycle: LifecycleCoordinator` on the mixin. That file, as a typecheck root, carries **37 pre-existing errors** of its own (the mixin also reads `settings`, `transport`, `_live_jev_available` from its composer). Touching it would have traded 17 inherited errors for 37 and made the leg worse - this issue's own mechanism, one level up. The declaration went on the test subclass that supplies the coordinator instead, and `task_lifecycle.py` is untouched.
